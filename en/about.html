Mission & vision

We believe parking should not be a gamble.

Mission

To remove the uncertainty of parking: connect drivers with parking lots so every spot is put to use and every trip ends without circling around.

Vision

To be the most trusted and simplest way to book parking in Latin America.

What drives us

  • Transparency

    Clear prices and conditions before you book.

  • Trust

    We review parking lots and look after your data.

  • Simplicity

    If it takes more than a few seconds, we are doing it wrong.

  • Local partners

    We grow alongside the parking lot owners of every city.
