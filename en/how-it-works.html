Step by step

Book in seconds

From the map to your saved spot in four steps. Tap each step to see it.

In total under a minute

  1. Open the map or the list and see which parking lots have space now, with their hourly price.

  2. Choose today or a future date and how long. For another day, the minimum is 2 hours. You see the total price before you continue.

  3. Sign in with your Apple or Google account in one tap. You fill in your vehicle details afterwards.

  4. Confirm your booking before 15 minutes remain until the start, or it is released. When you arrive, check in and you are done.

Quick questions

Do I need to create an account?

You just sign in with your Apple or Google account to save your spot. You fill in your details and your vehicle's after you book.

Can I book for another day?

Yes. You can book for today or a future date; for another day, the minimum is 2 hours.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Up to 15 minutes before the start time you cancel at no cost and we refund what you paid.

What if I do not confirm?

You must confirm your booking before 15 minutes remain until the start. Otherwise, the spot is released for someone else.

How do I pay?

You can pay in advance from the app with Mercado Pago, or settle it at the parking lot.

Does par-king look after my vehicle?

par-king is a booking platform. Custody of the vehicle is the responsibility of the parking lot.
